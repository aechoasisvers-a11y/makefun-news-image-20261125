# The world on Wednesday 25 November 2026 — six stories, one image

[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261125/aechoasisvers-a11y/makefun-news-image-20261125/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/competitions/news-image-20261125)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261125/aechoasisvers-a11y/makefun-news-image-20261125/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/competitions/news-image-20261125)

A 1024×1024 poster drawn entirely by pure-stdlib Python 3.13 (`main.py`:
its own 5×7 bitmap font, its own minimal PNG encoder — no Pillow, no numpy).
It sums up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in six stories, all first reported
that day:

1. **China launches Shenzhou 24** — three astronauts head for the Tiangong
   space station, including Pakistan's Zeeshan Ali, the first foreign crew
   member on board. (AP)
2. **Australia's apology to LGBTQ veterans** — Canberra formally apologises
   for decades of discrimination against gay and lesbian service members.
   (Reuters)
3. **Asian stocks tumble on a tariff threat** — shares fall across Asia as
   Washington prepares new duties on China, the EU and 14 other economies.
   (Reuters)
4. **Huang apologises to Nvidia staff** — a day after his return as chief,
   Jensen Huang says sorry, says he is in therapy and needs no succession
   plan. (CNBC / CNN)
5. **Iowa blasts 5 tons of explosive today** — the Army detonates 68 barrels
   of unstable lead azide at 9 a.m.; residents told to leave or stay indoors.
   (CNN / USA Today)
6. **Australian inflation runs hot** — prices rose more than expected in
   October, with core inflation at 3.8%, piling pressure on the Reserve Bank.
   (Reuters)

Run it with `python3 main.py` — it writes `/output/entry.png`
(pass an output path as the first argument to write elsewhere).
The output is deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical PNGs.
